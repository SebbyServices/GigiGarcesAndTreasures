Meet Gigi

Every treasure has a story

GigiG and Treasures started at my kitchen table with a handful of beads, a coil of wire, and the idea that jewelry should feel personal.

Every piece is still made the same way — by hand, in small batches, with materials I would wear myself. Nothing is mass-produced, and no two pieces are exactly alike.
