Meet Gigi

Every treasure has a story

GigiG and Treasures started at my kitchen table with a handful of beads, a coil of wire, and the idea that jewelry should feel personal.

Every piece in this shop is still made the same way. By hand, in small batches, with materials I would wear myself. Nothing is mass-produced, and no two pieces are exactly alike. When you wear a GigiG treasure, you are wearing something one person made just for you.
