Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

GigiG & Treasures ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have.

What we collect

This is a portfolio and catalog website — we do not operate a store, collect payments, or require account registration. The only personal information we receive is what you choose to send us:

  • Email inquiries: when you email us at {{ site.email }}, we receive your name, email address, and the content of your message.
  • Instagram messages: when you contact us via Instagram DM, that conversation is governed by Instagram's own privacy policy.

What we do not collect

  • We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, or behavioral advertising.
  • We do not use cookies of our own. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may set minimal cookies; we have no access to or control over those.
  • We do not collect payment information of any kind.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests a font file, Google receives your IP address and browser details. See Google's privacy policy for details.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (Microsoft). GitHub may log request metadata such as IP addresses for security and operations purposes.

How we use your information

We use email and Instagram messages only to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell, share, or rent your contact information to any third party.

Data retention

Email messages are retained in our Gmail inbox until deleted. Instagram messages are retained on Instagram's platform according to their policy.

Your rights

You may request that we delete any email correspondence you have sent us. Contact us at {{ site.email }} and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
