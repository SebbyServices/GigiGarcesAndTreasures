The Maker

Meet Gigi

GigiG and Treasures started at my kitchen table in 2019 with a jar of spare beads, a coil of wire, and an idea that jewelry should feel personal. Not mass-produced. Not designed by algorithm. Made by a person, for a person.

The first piece I ever finished was a simple stretch bracelet — turquoise and gold, sized for my wrist. I wore it everywhere. People kept stopping me to ask where I got it. When I told them I made it, they asked if I could make one for them.

That was the beginning.

The Studio

How every piece is made

Sourced with Intention

Every bead, stone, and finding is chosen by Gigi for its color, weight, and quality. Nothing is bought in bulk from anonymous suppliers.

Made by Hand

No molds, no machines, no outsourcing. Every clasp is set and every end cap is finished at the work table in Gigi's studio.

Inspected Before It Ships

Gigi inspects every piece herself before it leaves the studio. If it doesn't meet her standard, it doesn't ship.

The Philosophy

Why I make what I make

There are a lot of places to buy jewelry. Most of them sell things made in factories by people paid almost nothing, sold at enormous markups by brands spending millions to make you believe the piece has a story.

I make things that actually have a story, because I'm the one who made them. When you buy a GigiG piece, you know exactly who made it, what it's made of, and that someone cared enough to inspect it before it reached you.

That's not a marketing line. That's just how it works here.
